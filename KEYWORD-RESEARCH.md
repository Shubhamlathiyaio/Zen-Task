# Zen Task — SEO Keyword Research (2026)

Research date: Sep 2026. Market, competitor, and keyword notes for growing Zen Task organically.

## Positioning Summary

**The gap we exploit:** Competitors and roundup articles all say the same thing about Habitica
(the 4M+ user market leader): *dated/cluttered UI, no focus timer, requires internet, $4.99/mo*.
The new 2026 leader MainQuest wins on "modern + ADHD-friendly + free focus timer." Zen Task's
differentiators (beautiful dark theme, XP/rewards/levels, focus timer, parties, Eisenhower matrix,
Flutter mobile app) directly answer these complaints.

**Competitors to study:**
| App | Strength | Known Weakness (our angle) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Habitica | Brand/community (4M+ users) | Dated pixel UI, no focus timer, no offline, subscription |
| MainQuest | Modern RPG + SEO dominance | Younger brand, less community history |
| Finch | Gentle self-care pet | Not task/productivity focused |
| Forest | Focus timer game | Focus only, no tasks/habits |
| TickTick | All-in-one productivity | Gamification is shallow ("pointsification") |

## Keyword Clusters

### Tier 1 — Head terms (high volume, hardest; long-term goal)
- gamified to-do list app / gamify to-do list
- gamified habit tracker
- gamify your life
- gamified productivity app

### Tier 2 — Roundup / comparison terms (medium difficulty, high value)
- Habitica alternative / best Habitica alternatives
- best gamified habit tracker 2026
- best free habit tracker app
- RPG task manager
- gamified study app

### Tier 3 — Feature keywords (lower difficulty, high intent — win here first)
- focus timer with rewards / gamified pomodoro
- Eisenhower matrix app / priority matrix app
- reward system for chores / self reward app for tasks
- habit tracker with XP / habit tracker with levels
- accountability party app / stay accountable with friends
- ADHD task app / ADHD friendly to-do app with rewards

### Tier 4 — Problem / intent keywords (best for blog content, best conversion)
- can't stick to a to-do list
- how to make chores fun / how to make studying fun
- reward yourself for completing tasks (self-reward system)
- procrastination apps that actually work
- streak tracker / motivational habit app

## Recommended Content Snippets (one per keyword, for on-page SEO)
- Homepage hero should mention: **gamify your daily tasks, earn XP, level up, streaks, focus timer, play with friends** — all searchable terms users actually type.
- Blog angle #1: "Habitica alternatives in 2026" (comparison posts are earning the most traffic in this niche).
- Blog angle #2: "What makes a habit tracker fun" / "Why to-do lists fail" (Tier 4 intent keywords).
- Blog angle #3: "Gamified Pomodoro / focus timer that rewards you" (Tier 3, nobody owns it).
